# Research data boundary

Version `research-governance-1`. See [operating plan](data-access/DATA_GOVERNANCE.md), [access status and six eligibility questions](data-access/ACCESS_STATUS.md), [Shape Up! dossier](data-access/SHAPE_UP_REQUEST.md), [Fenland dossier](data-access/FENLAND_REQUEST.md), and [prospective protocol](REAL_USER_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md).

No restricted/raw longitudinal data have been obtained. No real DeltaShape model, fitted Pseudo-DXA dependency or external-user validation data are part of this repository. Synthetic fixtures are code-test material only.

The research tools run offline and have no application storage dependency. Their real-data gate requires documented analysis permission, understood derivative rights, secure external storage, dataset inventory and participant-safe split. A gate cannot infer legal permission. Application examples deny access until an accountable reviewer supplies private evidence.

Research cache/registered shapes/latents and study exports remain sensitive, even after removing names. They are excluded from Git and web assets. The deletion tool handles only a marked, enumerated cache root and defaults to a preview; source data and institutional backups require the steward's separate approved procedure.

The user has directed that applications must not be submitted now. Applicant organisation, official researcher, email, IRB/ethics, partner and storage country remain unfilled. The current Fenland ordinary research route requires institutional capacity the project has not established; its possible commercial route must be clarified rather than assumed impossible or approved.

Product facts, estimates, Avatar-derived measurements, forecasts and renders remain distinct. No research estimate or generated image is an observed fact or calibration outcome. Managed/photorealistic rendering and provider/GPU/API work are deferred.
