| Durable save | Сохраняем замер… / Обновляем модель и сохраняем данные… |

No “accuracy %”, medical precision claims, automatic cloud/account language or hidden uploads. Human measurements, photo estimates and visual corrections have distinct source labels. Unknown is “Нет данных” or “неизвестно”, never 0. Provider IDs, hashes and version strings are technical details, not ordinary product labels. Exact typed reasons and state gates remain authoritative; translated phrases are never parsed to determine behavior.
